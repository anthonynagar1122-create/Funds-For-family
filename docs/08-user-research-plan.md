# 08 — User research plan

## Objective
Confirm that families and friends will request and fund small loans through a trusted circle, and identify what trust signals they need.

## Questions
1. Who asks for small family or community loans today, and how (cash, messages, bank apps)?
2. What makes a lender comfortable: knowing the borrower, verified purpose, a named nominee, or the repayment date?
3. Would people pay attention to a feed of requests from their network? What makes them ignore one?
4. How do borrowers and nominees understand interest after the due date?
5. Are people willing to share contact lists to find their network, and what worries them?

## Method
| Method | Sample | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interviews (borrowers, lenders, nominees) | 6–8 | 2 weeks | Journey maps, quotes |
| Usability test on a prototype (request, verify, fund, repay) | 5–6 | 1 week | Task success, failure points |
| Short survey on trust signals | 100+ | 1 week | Ranked priorities |

## Interview guide (45 min)
- Warm-up (5): how do you usually help family with money?
- Context (10): last time someone asked you for money. What happened next?
- Deep dive (20): walk me through what would make you say yes. What would make you say no?
- Reaction (8): show the request detail with the nominee and interest rule. What do you understand?
- Wrap-up (2): anything we missed?

## Recruiting and ethics
- Recruit from the team's own network, avoiding anyone who would be a direct borrower or lender in the pilot.
- Obtain consent for recording. Do not collect financial details beyond what the study needs.
- Store notes without names; use participant codes.

## Success criteria for the pilot
- 5 of 6 usability participants complete a request and understand the interest rule without help.
- Clear top three trust signals identified.
