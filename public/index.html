How it works

Four steps from request to repayment

  1. 1

    Request

    Enter the amount, the purpose, the repayment date and a nominee from your network.

  2. 2

    Verify

    Verifiers review the purpose and record their decision. Two approvals open the request. Any rejection stops it.

  3. 3

    Fund

    Members contribute any amount, up to the remaining balance. The request is marked funded once the full amount is raised.

  4. 4

    Repay

    The borrower or nominee records repayments. The request is marked repaid when the full amount due is paid.

Interest, worked through

Interest starts after the due date, not before

Interest is simple, at 12% a year, and accrues one day at a time. Nothing is charged while the repayment is on time.

Questions

Questions people ask before lending

Does Funds For Family move money?

No. The app records requests, verifications, contributions and repayments. Any transfer of money happens outside the app, and that part is not yet connected to a payment provider.

Who checks the purpose of a request?

A verification team reviews each request. A request opens for funding only after two distinct approvals. A single rejection closes it. A verifier cannot decide on a request where they are the borrower or the nominee.

What is a nominee?

A nominee is a member named on the request who agrees to repay if the borrower does not. The borrower picks the nominee from their connections in the app.

When does interest start?

Interest starts the day after the repayment date. It is simple interest at 12% a year, accrued daily, and the amount due is always shown on the request.

Can I contribute any amount?

Yes, up to the balance still needed. You cannot contribute to your own request, and a request stops accepting money once it is fully funded.
