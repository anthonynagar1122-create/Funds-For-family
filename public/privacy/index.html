Privacy notice

Draft text. This page is a draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It may change.

What we keep

Your name, email address, optional UPI ID and photo from your sign-in, the requests you make, the payments you record, and the messages you send us for help.

Who can see what

  • People in your circle (you follow each other) can see your name and the approved requests you make.
  • A lender and the borrower can see each other's UPI ID for that payment. Nobody else sees it.
  • A trusted contact sees the request they are named on. A verifier sees the requests they check.
  • The owner of the service can see accounts and requests to run it and to answer help messages.
  • We do not sell your details and we do not show advertising.

How long we keep records

Requests and payment records are kept while the service runs, so both sides keep an accurate history. The saved view on your phone stays on your phone and is cleared when you sign out.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete account. We remove your name, email and UPI ID. Past payments stay in other people's records under "Former member". Deletion is blocked while money is owed either way.
