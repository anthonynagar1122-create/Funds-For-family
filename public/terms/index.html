What Funds For Family is

Funds For Family is a record of requests, verifications and repayments between people who know and trust each other. No money moves through the app. The app never holds, sends or guarantees money. Payments go straight between people, usually by UPI, and each side confirms them here.

The rules

  • Ask for up to ₹50,000 at a time and repay within 12 months.
  • There is no interest. You pay back what you borrowed.
  • A payment counts once the person receiving it confirms it.
  • Be truthful about why you are asking. A small team checks each request before it is shown to your circle.

Trusted contact

A request names a trusted contact, a person who knows the borrower and agrees to be named. The trusted contact is not a guarantor and has no obligation to repay. Only the borrower owes the money.

Your account

Funds For Family is invite only. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account, unless money is still owed either way. See the privacy notice for what happens to your details.

What we cannot do

We cannot collect a repayment for you or make anyone pay. The app is a shared record that helps people keep their word.
