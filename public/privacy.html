Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What we collect

  • Sign-in details from our sign-in provider (Clerk): your name, email address and a sign-in identifier.
  • Phone number, if you add one. Lenders use it to reach you. Only people you follow can see it, and only when they can also see one of your requests.
  • Connections: who you follow and who follows you.
  • Requests, contributions, repayments and verification notes that you create or make, which form the record of money lent and repaid.
  • Invitations you send, including any email address you invite.

How we use it

To run the family circle: showing requests to the right people, sending invitation and confirmation emails, and keeping an accurate record of each loan. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Audit record

We keep a record of sensitive actions (verifications, contributions, repayments, invitations accepted and account deletion), with your IP address, so disputes about who did what can be checked. When you delete your account, the IP addresses in those records are removed; the action records remain, linked to "Deleted member".

Who processes it

  • Clerk: sign-in and account identity.
  • Supabase: database storage.
  • Resend: sending emails.
  • Vercel: hosting the website and its server.

Deleting your account

In your profile, choose Delete my account. We then delete your sign-in identity and remove your name, email, phone number, connections and invitations. Requests, contributions and repayments remain, shown as "Deleted member", so that other members' records stay accurate. You cannot delete your account while you have an open or funded request, because it still has to be repaid.

Your rights

You can see and change your details in your profile, and delete your account as described above. If you live in India or the EU and want to ask about your data, contact us at [add contact email before launch].

Security

Personal data is stored in a database that is not public, and the server checks who is asking before it returns anything. No system is perfect, so if we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you as soon as we can.
